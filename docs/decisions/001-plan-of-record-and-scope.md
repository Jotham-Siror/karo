# 001 — Plan of record and scope
Date: 2026-10-19 · Supersedes: Karo — Build Plan.md (the baseline)

Joseph approved "Karo — Our Build Plan.md" as the plan of record after a review of the baseline plan.
- The doors are the web page and WhatsApp. Discord comes third, only if everything works by the Tue 08:00 feature freeze. Telegram, SMS and USSD go to the "what's next" slide.
- The PIN covers every payment out and every standing order, and is entered only on a one-time web page, never in chat.
- Relatives confirm their own share in the provider's checkout. Payments go out when confirmed, not on a future date.

Why: the baseline was too wide for about 30 hours, and parts of it contradicted each other. One example: "every money move needs the PIN" conflicted with relatives paying their own shares.
